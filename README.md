## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
Our proposed solution is a Digital Farmer Marketplace that connects farmers directly with consumers, retailers, hotels, supermarkets and wholesale buyers.

Farmers can register on the platform and upload their products with quantity, quality and price details. Consumers can view available products and order directly from farmers.

The platform reduces middlemen and helps farmers get better prices. It also helps consumers buy fresh agricultural products at affordable prices.

## Technical Approach

## Feasibility and Viability
The proposed solution is technically and economically feasible because it uses commonly available technologies.

Feasibility:
- Farmers can access the platform using smartphones.
- Buyers can easily search and order products.
- The system can be developed using affordable technologies.
- AI helps improve demand prediction and delivery planning
